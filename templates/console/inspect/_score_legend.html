How candidates are scored and found

score = w_sim·similarity + w_rec·recency + w_imp·decayed importance, with w_sim = {{ legend.similarity }}, w_rec = {{ legend.recency }}, w_imp = {{ legend.importance }} (this story's memory.episodic.weight_*). Importance is shown but is not itself a score term.

Similarity
how well the vector, lexical and alias arms agree, fused by reciprocal rank (0–1)
Recency
halves every recency_half_life_turns turns before the asked position ({{ legend.half_life }} for this story)
Decayed importance
importance halved over its age (the deployment's memory_importance_half_life_turns)
Importance
the memory's stored weight, shown for reference; it enters the score only as decayed importance

Episodes and assessments have no score, so their score columns read "—".
